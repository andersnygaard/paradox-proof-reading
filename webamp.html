The book in Webamp

Every narrated chapter as one playlist in Webamp, a copy of Winamp that runs in the browser. The list is read fresh each time this page opens, so a chapter that was narrated again plays in its new version. With the page open, press LOAD LIST in the playlist window to fetch the newest list.

Starting Webamp.

For Winamp, VLC or another player, use playlist.m3u8; it points at the mp3 files on this site. Webamp is made by Jordan Eldredge (MIT licence).
